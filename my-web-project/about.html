Application Blueprint & Guide

How to Use Digi Closet

1. Open the Generator workspace on a phone screen on a sunny morning.
2. Use your camera to catalog individual shirts, skirts, and pants.
3. Add items to your Wishlist Wardrobe to sketch out new fashion aesthetic paths safely without breaking budget restrictions.
